Why LEADATHON

A conventional hackathon asks "what can you code in 48 hours?" We ask something bigger.

"What important problem can you solve — and can you build something that proves it?" This isn't about slides. It's about working solutions that create measurable impact.

Beyond Coding

Hardware, AI, robotics, IoT, machinery, products or hybrid — build whatever the problem actually demands.

Local Problems, Global Potential

A solution built for one village or town can scale to a state, a nation, or the world.

Cross-Disciplinary

Students, professionals, farmers, designers and entrepreneurs — no single background owns innovation.

The Methodology

Five stages — from a problem that matters to impact the world can see.

01
IdentifyFind a problem that matters. Understand who it affects and why.
02
IdeateThink of a better way. Challenge assumptions, explore approaches.
03
InnovateBuild something real — a functional, tangible solution.
04
ImplementProve it works in a realistic environment.
05
ImpactMeasure the difference. Show who benefits and how it scales.
